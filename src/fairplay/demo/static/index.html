FairPlay: moderator-flag prioritisation

Research demo. Runs entirely in your browser: the same Python code and frozen model as the evaluated pipeline, loaded with Pyodide. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere except game links, which go straight to Lichess.

Research prototype. A score is a prioritisation signal, not evidence of wrongdoing.

      How to read this
      
    What this is. FairPlay ranks accounts for a moderator's
        review queue by how much their recent blitz games look like accounts that later
        carried a Lichess terms-of-service flag. It was trained on July 2025 and tested once on
        August 2025 (flag-precision 0.388 at a 0.5% weekly queue, 23× the base rate).
    
      
    What the label means. Would enter the review queue: the score is at or
        above the score of the last account admitted to the real August queue (median over
        weeks). It is a request for a human to look, never a verdict. A flag can mean any
        terms-of-service violation, not only engine use, and careful simulated engine use
        (strategy C) was near chance.
    
      
    Inputs. Lichess rated blitz games with clocks. Ten games within 30 days give an
        account score; fewer give only the per-game table. Player names are dropped on reading
        and nothing is stored.
